About the book

God Speaks to Me, Too is the testimony of a man whose life was marked by childhood violence, profound loss, and unmistakable encounters with God. It moves through decades of suffering, prayer, and spiritual awakening, including the loss of Patrick’s son Adam.

It is written for readers who have walked away from religion but not from God, and for those trying to locate hope inside grief.

About Patrick

Patrick J. Delohery is an electrical engineer and multidisciplinary product-development leader whose career spans consumer, industrial, and defense sectors, including work connected to NSA Headquarters and Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory.

He is also the founder of Epic Engineering Solutions in Colorado Springs, Colorado, and writes from lived experience rather than institutional religion.

30-day outreach plan

  1. Week 1: Publish the site, upload the media kit PDF, and add your Amazon links plus a short email signature with website and booking email.
  2. Week 2: Send 10 personalized podcast pitches and 10 church or ministry pitches. Use your 2-sheet PDF and interview topics for every outreach email.
  3. Week 3: Post 3 short story excerpts or quote graphics on LinkedIn and Facebook, then send 5 local media pitches focused on the Colorado Springs author angle.
  4. Week 4: Follow up with every non-response, ask readers for Amazon reviews, and add any interview mention or testimonial back onto this page as social proof.
