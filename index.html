God Speaks to Me, Too

A spiritual memoir about a 58-year, direct, literal relationship with God — tested by failure, grief, and silence, and kept with a precision that only one word describes: faithful.

This site is designed for spiritual-experience hosts, grief platforms, science-and-spirituality interviewers, memoir outlets, Colorado media, and selective post-evangelical or early-Christianity conversations where institutional religion is context rather than the headline.

Conversation angle: This is not a traditional Christian testimony. It is the account of a man who has spoken with God, Jesus, and the Holy Spirit — and who has had an Archangel appear at two decisive moments in his life, separated by thirty-three years. He is an electrical engineer. He believes in measurable signal. He is not asking anyone to take this on faith — he is asking them to listen to a witness.

“God did not abandon me. He withdrew. Those are different things.”

About the book

God Speaks to Me, Too begins this way: God has spoken to me. Jesus has spoken to me. The Holy Spirit has moved through me several times. An Archangel has stood beside me more than once. This is the account of 58 years of my direct, ongoing, and personal relationship with God — told plainly, by a man who has been held accountable with precision, forgiven with mercy, and carried through the unsurvivable by the love and the Grace of God.

I am not writing a theology. I am not starting a movement. I am simply telling you what has happened to me, in my life. If you have walked away from religion but not from God — this is for you. If you are sitting in grief right now, wondering where He is — this is especially for you. I know, with certainty, that God speaks to you, too.

About Patrick

Patrick J. Delohery is an electrical engineer with a 35+ year career across consumer, industrial, and defense sectors — including system integration work at NSA Headquarters and Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory while on assignment from Honeywell. He is a named inventor on multiple U.S. and foreign patents; his first microchip design was deployed in more than 5 million systems around the world.

He is the founder of EPIC Engineering Solutions in Colorado Springs, Colorado.
